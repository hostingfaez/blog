Dubai started as a small fishing and trading village by the creek, inhabited mostly by Bedouin tribes. For centuries, its economy relied on fishing, pearl diving, and trade with nearby regions.

In the early 20th century, the discovery of oil changed everything. With oil wealth, Dubai invested heavily in infrastructure, modern buildings, roads, and ports. The city attracted international business, turning it into a global trade and tourism hub.

Dubai’s leaders focused on innovation, building landmarks like the Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah, and the Museum of the Future. The city became known for mixing tradition with futuristic development, making it a symbol of rapid growth and ambition.
