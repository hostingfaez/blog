### Race Day

So i did my first sprint triathlon in Lumut, Perak on 13 June 2025 and it was great ( bolehla ) . Got 19th in age group category  and top 60 for overall for sprint category. Untuk swim dia takde ombak tapi current dia sangat kuat and i was struggling to get the tempo walaupun HR utk swim aku z2.  

Cam bila tgk buoy yg depan tu rasa cam jauhnya bila la nak sampai and utk bike pulak it was a flat course and banyak pr hahaha and got warned once dengan marshall sbb blocking utk potong sbb berborak dgn cina tu and said sorry to each other back.

Untuk run dah start panas sangat and start mental lagi sbb loop 2 kali loop and nasib baik dia simbah air sejuk nak mampus aku rasa tu ais yg dah cair.
