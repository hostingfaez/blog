> ***Located in Sheikh Zayed Road.***

![[Untitled 10.jpeg]]

1. **Torus shape (ring with no corners) means continuous innovation.**
The building’s smooth, round design shows that innovation never stops and keeps moving forward without limits or edges.
2. **The empty middle (void) represents the unknown future.**
This space is left open and mysterious to remind us that the future holds many things we have yet to discover and understand.
3. **The grass around the museum reminds us that Earth is our home.**
The natural green area connects the museum’s futuristic ideas to the importance of taking care of our planet.
4. **The quote says: “The future belongs to those who imagine and make it real. No need to wait 1000 years.”**
This encourages everyone to dream big and start creating the future now, instead of waiting for it to happen later.
